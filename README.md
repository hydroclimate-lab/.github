# ICARUS HydroClimate Lab

Welcome to the **ICARUS HydroClimate Lab**, Department of Geography, Maynooth University.

We undertake research in hydroclimate science, reproducible environmental data science, and open-source software to improve understanding and prediction of water systems across Ireland, Europe and globally.

---

## Research Themes

- Catchment hydrology
- Hydroclimatology
- Floods and droughts
- Hydrological forecasting
- Seasonal prediction
- Environmental data science
- GIS and Earth observation
- Reproducible research

---

## Teaching

The HydroClimate Lab supports:

- MSc research
- PhD training
- Python workshops
- Reproducible computational research

---

## Principles

We aim to develop research that is:

- Reproducible
- Collaborative
- Well documented
- Open where possible
- Scientifically robust
